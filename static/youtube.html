Write Your Script

A

Use a Hook in the First 5 Seconds

Start with a bold claim, a shocking statistic, or a direct question. YouTube shows your video to 2–5% of subscribers — a great hook determines if they keep watching.

Example: "Most people will never know this secret that…"
B

Structure: Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA

Keep it tight. 8–12 minutes is the sweet spot for mid-roll ads. Write ~1,400–1,800 words — this converts to roughly 9–11 minutes of audio at a normal pace.

C

Write for the Ear, Not the Eye

Short sentences. No tables or lists that won't translate to audio. Conversational language. Avoid jargon unless your niche demands it. Read it aloud before pasting.

D

Limit to 5,000 Characters per Request

Twelve Labs processes up to 5,000 characters at a time. For longer scripts, split into logical segments (intro, point 1, point 2, outro) and generate each separately.

Tip: Split at natural paragraph breaks for seamless joins
⚠️Avoid rushing the script. The voiceover quality is only as good as the script. Spend 60% of your time here.

Generate Voiceover with Twelve Labs

Open the Twelve Labs app at your local URL and follow these exact steps:

Step 1 — Paste your script segment

Paste the first segment (up to 5,000 characters) into the text area. Watch the character counter in the top-right of the input panel.

Step 2 — Select the right voice

See the voice table below for recommended voices per niche. Each voice carries a distinct tone — match it to your audience.

Step 3 — Set Speed to +10% to +20%

AI voices can sound sluggish at default speed. A slight boost (+10%) sounds more energetic and keeps watch time higher.

Step 4 — Leave Pitch at 0 Hz

Pitch changes can introduce artifacts on neural voices. Only adjust if the voice sounds unnatural for your niche.

Step 5 — Click "Generate Voice"

Wait for the audio player to appear. The orange waveform will animate during playback. Listen through the full segment before downloading.

Step 6 — Download the MP3

Click Download MP3. The file saves as twelvelabs_audio.mp3. Rename it to match your segment (e.g., intro.mp3, part1.mp3).

Step 7 — Repeat for each segment

Generate all script segments. In your video editor, join them in order. Most editors auto-remove tiny silences between clips.

Build the Video

1

Import all MP3 segments into your editor

Recommended tools: CapCut (free, beginner-friendly), DaVinci Resolve (free, professional), or Adobe Premiere. Join segments in sequence on the audio track.

2

Add B-Roll / Stock Footage

Use Pexels.com or Pixabay (free) or Storyblocks (paid). Match visuals to every sentence — viewers leave when visuals don't match audio.

Rule: New clip every 3–5 seconds for max retention
3

Add Captions / Subtitles

70% of YouTube viewers watch with subtitles. Use CapCut's auto-caption feature or upload the MP3 to Whisper AI for a free SRT file. Bold key words in the captions.

4

Add Background Music

Use YouTube Audio Library (royalty-free) or Uppbeat.io. Keep music at -20 to -25 dB below the voiceover. Never use trending copyrighted music.

5

Export Settings

Export at 1920×1080 (Full HD) or 3840×2160 (4K). H.264 codec, 8–15 Mbps bitrate. YouTube recommends MP4 format.

Audio: AAC, 320 kbps stereo
5

Optimize & Upload

T

Title — Use This Formula

Format: [Emotional hook] + [Specific benefit/number] + [Curiosity gap]

Example: "The Dark Truth About Passive Income Nobody Talks About"
D

Description — First 3 Lines Matter Most

YouTube shows only the first 2–3 lines before "Show more". Put your main keyword and a compelling hook here. Add timestamps, links, and keywords in the full description.

T

Thumbnail — The #1 Click Driver

Use Canva (free) with high contrast backgrounds, bold text (max 4 words), and a dramatic image. Test your thumbnail at small size — it must be readable on mobile.

T

Tags & Chapter Markers

Add 5–10 relevant tags. Create chapter markers in the description using timestamps — this adds a table of contents to your video and improves search visibility.

🕐Best upload times: Tuesday–Thursday between 2–4 PM (your audience's timezone). Avoid Mondays and weekends for new channels.

Monetization Strategies

📺 YouTube Partner Program

Requires 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views). Earn $2–$28 RPM depending on niche. Enable mid-roll ads on videos 8+ minutes.

🛒 Affiliate Marketing

Mention relevant products in your script and add affiliate links to the description. Finance and tech channels earn $50–$200+ per referral on some programs.

📦 Sponsorships

At 10K subscribers, brands may approach you. Use Grapevine or AspireIQ to find sponsors. Rates: $200–$2,000 per video depending on niche and views.

💎 Digital Products

Sell eBooks, courses, or templates related to your niche via Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy. This is often more profitable than ad revenue long-term.

📋YouTube's AI-generated content policy: You must disclose that your content uses AI-generated voiceover in the upload settings. Failure to do so can result in demonetization.

Scale & Automate

1

Batch Generate Scripts with AI

Use ChatGPT or Claude to generate 5–10 script outlines at once for a chosen topic cluster. Review and edit, then process them through Twelve Labs in a queue.

Target: 3–5 videos per week for rapid channel growth
2

Use the Batch API Endpoint

Twelve Labs exposes a /api/batch endpoint. Send multiple text segments in one request and get all audio files returned together — ideal for processing full scripts.

POST /api/batch → returns multiple MP3 blobs
3

Build a Content Pipeline

Script → Twelve Labs voiceover → CapCut auto-edit → Canva thumbnail → Schedule upload via YouTube Studio. Once this pipeline is set, each video takes ~30 minutes.

4

Repurpose Content Across Platforms

Export short clips (30–60 sec) for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. The same voiceover + different visuals = 3x the content from one script.

Shorts require 9:16 vertical format
5

Analyse & Double Down

After 20 videos, check YouTube Analytics. Find your top 3 videos by CTR and watch time. Create 10 more videos on those exact topics. The algorithm rewards consistency in a topic cluster.

🚀Growth benchmark: Consistent creators in high-RPM niches using AI voice tools hit 1,000 subscribers within 60–90 days. Post at least 3 videos per week in the first month.
